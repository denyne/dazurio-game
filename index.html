A GAME FOR CURIOUS MINDS

Small questions.
Big discoveries.

DAZURIO is a daily guessing game built around curiosity, learning and the joy of getting surprisingly close.

One question can take you anywhere.

From geography and science to history, nature, technology and everyday life, DAZURIO challenges you to think, estimate and discover something new.

Seven questions make one daily challenge. Everyone playing the same version gets the same daily set.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got answers.

How many questions are in a daily game?

Each daily challenge contains seven questions.

How much time do I have?

You have 25 seconds to submit each answer.

How is my score calculated?

Your score depends on how close your estimate is to the correct answer.

Can I play previous challenges?

Yes. Visit the Archive to replay previous daily sets.

Does DAZURIO have online accounts?

This prototype stores profiles and game data locally in your browser. It does not use online accounts.

Is DAZURIO Plus a real subscription?

No. In this prototype, Plus is a local preview and does not process payments.
